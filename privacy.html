Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PhantomPlays Bot processes, how that information is used, and what choices users have.

1. Information We Process

Depending on which features are used, PhantomPlays may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, and channel IDs.
  • Message activity counts used by the analytics feature.
  • Warning and moderation records created through moderation commands.
  • Information and text submitted through bot commands, such as translation requests.
  • AFK information provided by users when using AFK features.

2. Analytics

PhantomPlays uses an analytics system that records server IDs, channel IDs, member IDs, and message counts to provide usage statistics.

The analytics records described above contain activity counts and identifiers, not the text of every message.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Provide bot commands and features.
  • Operate moderation and warning systems.
  • Provide analytics and usage statistics.
  • Manage AFK and ticket-related features.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the bot.

4. Third-Party Services

PhantomPlays operates through Discord's platform and API. Discord processes information according to its own Privacy Policy.

When the translation feature is used, submitted text may be sent to Google Translate through the translation service used by the bot. That service is subject to its own policies.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Some bot records, including analytics and moderation information, are stored in a database on the system hosting PhantomPlays.

Information is kept for as long as it is needed to operate the bot, provide its features, and maintain relevant records. The exact retention period may depend on the type of information and the bot's configuration.

6. Data Sharing

PhantomPlays does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and third-party services when needed to provide bot features.

The bot operator may access stored records for maintenance, moderation, and support purposes.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using PhantomPlays by asking a server administrator to remove the bot from the server or by no longer interacting with it.

For questions or requests about stored information, contact the PhantomPlays operator using the official support contact below. Requests will be reviewed and handled where reasonably possible.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored bot information. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PhantomPlays changes its features or data practices. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
